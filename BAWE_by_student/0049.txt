Circa 2000 BC the Babylonians developed a formula for solving the quadratic polynomial equation . Since that time , mathematical history is littered with successful and failed attempts to solve poly-nomials of higher degrees . In the four thousand years since the Babylonians solved the quadratic , the cubic and quartic were solved towards the end of the Dark Ages and the Renaissance respec-tively and there were numerous researches into polynomials . Then Abel and Galois independently proved the unsolvability of the quintic by radicals , early in the nineteenth century .
