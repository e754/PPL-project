In order to successfully implement manufacturing strategy , a clear understanding of all the forces for or against decisions is vital . The force field diagram , which was developed by Kurt Lewin , is a simple but very effective tool to weigh pros and cons . Through drawing a diagram , it is easy to see the forces for and against the change , and identify whether the forces for change will become dominant . Once one decision to carry out a project has been made , Force Field Analysis is helpful to work out how to improve its probability of success . Usually , there are three choices . First , increase or add new driving force . Second , reduce or remove the forces against the change . Third , change the direction of forces .
