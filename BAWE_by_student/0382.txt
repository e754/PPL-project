Effect of Changing Ligand : This can be determined from comparison of Polymer 5A and 5B as these reactions involved using the exact same procedure however the only difference was that of the metal binding ligand involved . Polymer 5A involved using the propyl ligand whereas Polymer 5B uses the octyl ligand . Polymer 5B had the smaller rate constant of 0.000412 compared to Polymer 5A which had a rate constant of 0.000536 . This difference in the rate of reaction must be due to the respective sizes of the two ligands . The propyl ligand is small and has less steric hindrance , whereas the octyl ligand is large and bulky , therefore has a large steric hindrance and as a result reduces the rate of the reaction . Effect of Changing the Monomer : This can be seen by comparison of the rate of the reaction for Polymer 5A and 5E as both have the same experimental procedure however use different monomers . Polymer 5A involves the polymerisation of methyl methacrylate -LRB- MMA -RRB- whereas Polymer 5E polymerises the monomer butyl methacrylate -LRB- BMA -RRB- . Polymer 5E has the smaller rate constant of 0.000280 compared to Polymer 5A which has a rate constant of 0.000536 . The difference in rate constants is due to the size of each of the monomers . Polymer 5A uses the smaller MMA monomer which has the smaller methoxy group and so a smaller steric hindrance whereas Polymer 5E uses the larger BMA monomer that has the large and bulky butyloxy group attached which carries a larger steric hindrance and so reduces the rate of the reaction . Effect of time of addition : The effect of this can be seen from comparing the rate of reactions of Polymer 5C and 5D . The reaction for Polymer 5C involved addition of benzyl methacrylate -LRB- BzMA -RRB- to an already forming MMA polymer after 2 hours to form the block co-polymer . Whereas Polymer 5D involved addition of BzMA to an already forming MMA polymer after 4 hours . Changing the addition time should have a slight effect on the rate of the reaction . Polymer 5C involves adding the BzMA 2 hours earlier than that of Polymer 5D . As BzMA is larger than MMA it has a larger steric hinderance and so the longer BzMA is in the reaction mixture the more effect it has on reducing the rate of the reaction , therefore Polymer 5C should have a slower rate of reaction as it had the BzMA in the reaction solution 2 hours earlier than Polymer 5D .
