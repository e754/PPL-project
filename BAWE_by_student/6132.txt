Nanotechnology is a science combining chemistry and engineering by manipulating materials at atomic level . Nanotechnology is performed on a tiny scale The word nano comes from the Greek word nanos , meaning dwarf . A nanometer is one billionth of a meter -LRB- 10 -9 m -RRB- an atom is about 0.1 to 0.2 nm in diameter and a human hair is 80,000 nm in wide . Nanomaterials are usually about 0.1 to 100 nanometres in size this is not just referring to manmade structures but to those which exist in nature . For example DNA is about 2nm wide and in contrast a red blood cell is 7000nm across . Nanotechnology is used today in a broad range of applications and has vast potential for improvement of existing technologies for the future . The development of the catalytic converter shows haw nanoscience has already benefited our world by reducing the harmful emissions of our car exhausts . With the use of nanotechnology tiny nanomachines could be developed to travel through the blood stream or lymphatic system to directly target tumours , clear blood clots or close wounds ; this would transform surgery making it safer and more effective . Further development of nanoscience in the environmental fields could lead to smaller more efficient batteries and solar cells . Nanotechnology could revolutionaries computing by allowing smaller chips with more capacity and higher efficiency to be built . An example from an article by Ralph C. Merkle entitled `` Nanotechnology : It 's a Small , Small , Small , Small World '' describes how nanotechnology will enable logic elements to be constructed from just a few atoms , memory storage devices that could hold more than a hundred billion billion bytes will take only the volume of a sugar cube . Nanoscale engineering involves two different processes one , the traditional method of forming shapes called `` top-down '' and the new known as `` bottom-up '' . Top-down involves taking your raw material and cutting it down to the size and shape you desire , bottom-up involves taking each atom one by one and placing them together until you achieve the required structure . Bottom-up is a complicated process but it can be made easier as some materials such as nanowires can self assemble , the structures are simple and the atoms -LRB- carbon -RRB- naturally combine in these specific forms due to bonding preferences . In our cells DNA is reproduced to make new with the aid of proteins , it is hoped that similarly nanomachines may be developed that either self replicate or replicate other existing nanostructures to replace the old . However , there are some negative aspects to this technology , this is a new science and the past has demonstrated how science can lead in unpredictable and often undesirable directions . Little is known about the toxicological effects of nanoparticles or the effects of these on the natural world , they might react unfavourably with the nanoprocesses already present . It is well known that size especially at the nanoscale effects the physical and chemical properties of a material , for example gold used in glass making will colour the glass blue , red or gold depending on its fineness .
