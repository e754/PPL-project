This second researcher doing the evaluation will be blinded , that is , unaware of treatment allocation to avoid bias -LRB- Buckeldee and McMahon , 1994 -RRB- . The Arthritis Hand Function Test -LRB- AHFT -RRB- will be used to measure the variables of ROM and muscle strength . This test consists of 11 items designed to measure pure and applied strength and dexterity , and gives scores according to individuals ' ability -LRB- Backman and Mackie , 1995 -RRB- . The outcome measures will be recorded at baseline -LRB- in the first week of the programme -RRB- and at 1 week after completion of the 20 week programme . ` Other variables ' will be recorded at baseline only . A mean will be calculated for the age , gender and length of diagnosis of RA of the participants in each group , and the standard deviation -LRB- SD -RRB- given . SD will show the dispersion around the mean -LRB- Taylor , 2000 ; Greene and D'Olivera , 1999 -RRB- . This information will be supplied to enable the reader of the research to see these other factors which may have an impact on the results . For participants who may withdraw from the treatment , an ` intention-to-treat ' analysis will be undertaken , so all participants are accounted for -LRB- Bury and Mead , 1998 -RRB- . The results will be assessed by comparing outcomes in the treatment and control groups -LRB- Mulrow and Oxman , 1997 , cited in Bury and Mead , 1998 -RRB- . The data produced will be ordinal data , which can be put into an order . For example , one score from the AHFT can be better or worse than another score . A non-parametric test , the Mann Witney U test will be used to determine is there is a statistically significant difference between the control group and the treatment group . -LRB- See table 2 . -RRB- -LRB- Greene and D'Olivera , 1999 ; Robson , 2002 -RRB- . This needs to take place to determine whether any differences in outcome between these 2 groups would have occurred by chance . That is , whether it can be certain that any improvement in the treatment group was due to the intervention or due to chance factors -LRB- Taylor , 2000 -RRB- . The level of significance is expressed in terms of the P-value -LRB- P = probability -RRB- . The smaller the P-value , the more confidence can be given that any differences are not due to chance -LRB- Taylor , 2000 -RRB- . A limitation of the before-and-after study design is that it measures total change , so it can not be determined whether independent or extraneous variables are responsible for producing the change in the dependent variable -LRB- Kumar , 1996 -RRB- . For example , individuals with RA may have disease exacerbation as the result of major psychological stressors -LRB- extraneous variable -RRB- , which would have an effect on the results . The nature of RA may also effect the results as there are various stages of RA -LRB- early , moderate , severe , terminal -RRB- and strength may vary depending on times of the day when joint stiffness is decreased -LRB- Turner , Foster and Johnson , 2002 -RRB- . It may therefore , depend on the time of day the AHFT is carried out as to what results are obtained , as well as the stage of RA each participant has .

The sample will consist of between 30 and 40 patients with RA of the hand who attend one of three hand clinics in and around a major city , to see the occupational therapist -LRB- OT -RRB- . The sampling technique will be ` homogenous sampling ' which explores a particular subgroup of people -LRB- Patton , 1990 , cited in Grbich , 1999 -RRB- . The sample size will be determined once saturation point within the data analysis has been reached , this is when further data collection appears to add no new information to that already gathered -LRB- Robson , 2002 ; Sim and Wright , 2000 -RRB- . The researcher will not be employed at the clinics to minimise bias . -LRB- Sim and Wright , 2000 -RRB- . Potential participants will be given an information sheet inviting them to participate in the study and explaining the nature of their participation -LRB- See ` Data collection ' -RRB- . Participants will be gathered over a period of 6 months to ensure a large enough number of participants can be obtained , until saturation point is reached -LRB- Robson , 2002 ; Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . Collecting data over a prolonged period can also ensure a range of participants are involved which will increase credibility -LRB- Taylor , 2000 -RRB- . Qualitative research is used to ` understand the meanings , experience , and phenomena as they evolve in the natural setting ' -LRB- Depoy and Gitlin , 1994 , cited in Taylor , 2000 , p101 -RRB- . The qualitative research approach to be used for this study is naturalistic , phenomenological research . This focuses on understanding and interpreting human experience -LRB- Depoy and Gitlin , 1998 -RRB- . Particular phenomena , events or experiences are explored in an attempt to understand an individuals ' lived experience -LRB- Spiegelberg , 1975 , cited in Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 ; Taylor , 2000 -RRB- . Crotty -LRB- 1996 , cited in Grbich , 1999 -RRB- gives 5 steps involved in the phenomenological research process . -LRB- See table 1 -RRB- . Naturalistic inquiry takes a holistic perspective and postulates that those who have the experiences are most knowledgeable about them -LRB- Depoy and Gitlin , 1998 -RRB- . Therefore , semi-structured interviews will be conducted , asking the patients themselves about their experiences . To increase credibility , data will be collected using a variety of research methods , and from different sources . This process in know as triangulation and can enhance the rigour of the research -LRB- Mays and Pope , 2000 ; Taylor , 2000 ; Robson , 2002 -RRB- . Therefore , three hand clinics are being included in this study rather than one , and in order to answer the research questions , three forms of data collection will be conducted : One to one semi-structured interviews with participants , conducted according to a list of topics to be covered in an interview guide . -LRB- See ` Research questions ' -RRB- . The interviews will be audio taped with the participants ' consent , and subsequently transcribed . Participants will be asked to keep a diary for 4 weeks , to keep a record of when/if they practice the exercise and splinting regime in their own time , and when/if they attend appointments to see the OT . They will also write about their feelings about their treatment related to appointments , the regime and daily life activities throughout the weeks . A researchers diary will be kept , to enable readers of the research to understand ` how the researcher thinks , acts , interacts with and feels about the participants in the project , and how the researchers concerns and values affect the data ' -LRB- Grbich , 1999 , p89 -RRB- . The suspension of beliefs , assumptions and biases about the phenomena under investigation is known as ` phenomenological reduction ' -LRB- Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . Interviews , and meetings with participants to look at diaries will take place in the particular hand clinic the participant attends , so that the environment is familiar to them -LRB- Mays and Pope , 2000 ; Sim and Wright , 2000 -RRB- . Participants will be invited to participate in one to one interviews with the researcher . The interviewer 's appearance should be similar to the interviewee 's so as not to come across as too authoritative , and the interviewer should try to make the interviewee comfortable in expressing their true experiences -LRB- Robson , 2002 ; Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . Semi-structured interviews are appropriate where a study focuses on the meaning of particular phenomena to the participants -LRB- King , 1994 , cited in Grbich , 1999 -RRB- ; they are therefore the most appropriate interview design for this study . Semi-structured interviews have predetermined questions , but the order in which they are asked can be modified based on what the interviewer feels is most appropriate . The wording , order and explanations of the questions can vary , and questions which seem inappropriate with a particular interviewee can be omitted , or additional ones added -LRB- Robson , 2002 -RRB- . The design of semi-structured interviews enables interviewees to elaborate on their answers , and the interviewer to ask probing questions , so comprehensive data can be obtained -LRB- See table 2 , ` The 4 Components of Asking ' -RRB- . In the initial interview , participants will be asked open questions about their attending of appointments and whether they follow the exercise and splinting regime , and in what ways they think it helps . Further and probing questions will be asked , depending on the answers given . Probes are neutral statements which do not bias the subject to respond in any particular way . They are used to encourage more information , or elaborations , for example , `` tell me more about it '' -LRB- Depoy and Gitlin , 1998 -RRB- . Summary questions are also useful at the end of an interview , to enable the interviewer to check they have understood the interviewee correctly -LRB- Grbich , 1999 -RRB- . The second interview will provide an opportunity to expand , verify and add descriptions to the phenomena being researched . The researcher can refer to limited or inadequately described information to gain further descriptions . Often , the participants will also have additional thoughts about the phenomena , following the initial interview -LRB- Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . The second interview will take place after the 4 weeks that the participant has kept a diary , and will also be an opportunity to discuss what is written in the diary . Audio taping is a fundamental data-recording strategy in naturalistic inquiry . Participants must be informed that they are being recorded , so they can give their consent , and they are also to be informed that confidentiality will be maintained , and that they can request the audio-taping to cease at any point -LRB- Depoy and Gitlin , 1998 -RRB- . Grbich -LRB- 1999 -RRB- notes that there is a tendency for respondents to engage in casual conversation on the topic , once the tape recorder has stopped , commenting reflectively on what they have just said . The author says that this ` off-the-record ' data often allows the interviewer to see what is behind the interviewee 's recorded responses , and the researcher should make a mental note of what had been said and write up these observations as soon as possible after the interview . However , doing this means that information is from the researcher 's memory , and not from a reliable source ; therefore it could have been misinterpreted and would lead to incorrect data . For the purpose of this study , this will not be recommended ; instead , the interviewer will be encouraged to write about any such events in their diary . The interviewer will transcribe the interviews verbatim from the audio tapes rather than during the interview . However , whilst in the interviews , notes will be kept of how the interviewee expresses themselves in other ways in relation to the questions being asked , for example , if they are upset , laughing , or demonstrating with their hands . Any relevant expressions will subsequently be added to the transcriptions to give further depth -LRB- Robson , 2002 ; Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . Data analysis begins when data collection begins ; they are inseparable processes -LRB- Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . Analysis will therefore take place throughout the research as an ongoing process -LRB- Taylor , 2000 -RRB- . Data analysis requires the researcher to become immersed in the data and in the phenomenon under investigation -LRB- Streubert and Rinaldi Carpenter , 1995 -RRB- . A strategy to ensure credibility and trustworthiness of the data will be to involve participants in the analysis of the research , by giving them access to the transcribed data , and also the researcher 's interpretations of it , and allowing them to comment on the research -LRB- Taylor , 2000 -RRB- . This is known as member checking -LRB- Mays and Pope , 2000 ; Robson , 2002 -RRB- . The data will be analysed using thematic analysis . The themes to be concentrated on will be those that relate to the participants feelings about the exercise and splinting regime they are currently undertaking . A descriptive account of the phenomena under investigation will be produced and a theoretical explanation given -LRB- Ritchie , Spencer and O'Connor , 2003 -RRB- . These results will give an answer to the research question . Peer evaluation , member checking and audit will be used to review the research process to ensure dependability and validity -LRB- Taylor , 2000 ; Robson , 2002 -RRB- . This kind of research can not be generalised .
