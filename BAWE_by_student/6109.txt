Encolpio , upon abandoning the old men eating the corpse of Eumolpo , gains colour on the fresco of the wall . He is complete , even though the other elements are not . This contrasts to the beginning scene where Encolpio is set against a plain wall , and for a period of time the only element we have of him is his voice . Even when he comes into view he is clearly a tormented person who is constantly in search of something . By the end of the film he has found peace by embracing life , even though he does not have his beloved Giton or companion Ascilto . Perhaps Fellini is trying to make his audience understand the value of life , and how it does not matter if we are unable to achieve or gain all we desire , for life is a wonderful thing that we should embrace and take pleasure in for its own sake . It can also be a warning , as Fellini is trying to show his audience that one day they will be part of the frescoes , lost and forgotten . He therefore urges the people watching the movie to make the most of their lives before it is taken from them . Religion is not an area which Fellini explores to a great extent . Ben Hur and Quo Vadis contrast the brutal Romans to the saintly Christians but Satyricon avoids such matters . Even though the film is set around the period of Nero , the Christian matter is never addressed . It could be argued that Eumolpo in the gallery scene is a saintly figure . He wears a simple toga that resembles that of a priest and he preaches of how to lead a better life . He guides others to be humble , to ignore the temptations of wealth and sex and develop our skills in the arts . He does not , however , advise us on faith but on the philosophical and artistic elements . He argues against those who have ignored poetic thought and discussions in favour for less intellectual elements . In this sense , he appears to resemble a Greek philosopher rather than a Christian priest . Male relationships are a clear focus within the film . In Spartacus a homosexual relationship was hinted at in the ` oysters and snails ' scene . In contrast to this Satyrcon embraces homosexual relationships fully and does not try to hide it behind the dialogue . Unlike Spartacus , it is not just the bad characters that are involved in sexually deviant relationships , but the majority of the cast . Encolpio and Ascilto may not be viewed as the typical male , but they are not cruel or nasty . They engage in the same sort of homosexual relationships as the undesirable Lichas . Homosexuality is not used to characterise or define a character , it is merely their preference . This is a better depiction of the past then that shown by previous films . It can be argued that the Greeks and Romans had no definition for homosexuality , so did not feel that it existed . It is likely that they viewed bisexuality as the norm , and I feel that this can be shown by Suetonius who commented on how Claudius did not like to go with men . Such a comment would assume it was unusual at the time not to have same-sex relations . If this is the case then Satryicon is clearly a more accurate reflection of the sexual relationships that occurred in antiquity . Baxter -LRB- 1993:243 -RRB- encapsulates this idea when he writes that `` it is a film about homosexuality , but with no feeling of sin or shame '' . The masculinity of the male is another focal point in the film . In contrast to the leads in Spartacus and Gladiator , Encolpio , Ascilto and Giton are feminine males , sporting none of the traditional Roman characteristics of a muscular body , chiselled bone structure and deep voice . All three wear revealing togas and are the opposite of what is typically desired . Whereas Maximus encapsulated the strong warrior , Encolpio is unable to fight the Minotaur and fails sexually as well . The closest he comes to being a traditional male is in his relationship with Giton , as when they are together Encolpio is the master and Giton lays on him , as a female would in most love scenes . Giton was apparently chosen for the role due to the fact he was `` small , pale , pouting and androgenous '' -LRB- Baxter 1993:243 -RRB- . The idea of the male body being on show is also developed through the film . This is like most films set in antiquity where the focus is on the naked elements of the male body , and how it is on display for the audience to enjoy . The scene where Lichas and Encolpio wrestle can be contrasted to the gladiatorial fight in Spartacus . In both there is a battle between two scantily dressed males , and they are watched not only by those in the film but also by the audience . There are a number of clear differences that reveal how Fellini attempts to show a difference in the way antiquity can be viewed . In Spartacus the men are muscular and the women prey over them , using them for their own sexual enjoyment . The gladiators do not want to fight but have to in order to live . Draba goes against the system and chooses death . In contrast Lichas wants to fight Encolpio , who at the beginning resists him but eventually gives in and kisses his component after a short moment of resistance . Lichas is an undesirable male with a false eye and flabby old body , whereas Encolpio is a feminine male that opposes the ideal , sexy gladiator . The spectacle of the male , therefore , is completely different . In Spartacus the bodies of the men is primarily for the gaze of the female audience , in Satyricon it is again aimed at a male audience and only Encolpio is desired . To enhance the view of the male body , Fellini only includes the female when it is necessary . To exaggerate the sexuality of the leading males , the women are rarely shown to be attractive when they are naked . The Widow and the woman that commits suicide are both pretty but fully dressed , and neither are involved in erotic scenes . The prostitutes in the brothels and the females in the banquet scene are depicted as garish tramps and it is impossible to find them attractive . Baxter -LRB- 1993:247 -RRB- shows this clearly when he states that `` The whores loll in a bath or lie on a slab with flames underneath . One has a big metal mirror over the bed . Another squats to piss on the earth floor . Any beauty is portrayed by an awkward pose , sweaty , unhealthy skin , a tongue-wobbling grimace or a leer . `` The women are not the beautiful , desirable females that we are used to . They immediately contrast to the women that we are used to viewing on the screen such as Cleopatra , whose power is created by her beauty and sexual appeal . The women in Satyricon have no right of their own and hold no power over the men ; they are merely tools used by the males in order for them to fulfil their goals . This can be best shown in the scene where Encolpio visits the group of women to cure himself from his impotence . These moderately attractive women fail and the woman that returns his sexual ability is the opposite to what men typically desire ; the Earth goddess is large , middle aged and with sagging breasts . She is the only woman with power in the film and goes against all that we usually associate with the strong and powerful female of antiquity . This again highlights Fellini 's point that our views may not be consistent that of the Romans , as the desirable woman of today may differ substantially to the type of woman sought after in antiquity . Imperialism is also touched upon . Lichas is the embodiment of Rome , as he is ' a roaming imperial pimp and homosexual pirate who enjoys wrestling his captives to death ' -LRB- Baxter 1993:247 -RRB- . He , like Rome , destroys every community he meets and ensures that they bow down to his desires , as Encolpio is shown doing after they wrestle . Like Rome , he removes their ideals in place of what the empire wants and kills all those who fight against it . The film does not go out to reject imperialism as other films have , but there is an underlying thought that the empire is not an ideal concept . Fellini -LRB- 1978:16 -RRB- wrote of the similarities that are shown in the film with an imperialistic nation ; `` a society in which politics is only the sordid , routine administration of a common affluence and an end in itself ; where big businesses intrudes at all levels in the brutality of its instruments and the vulgarity of its ends ; a society in which all beliefs - religious , philosophical , ideological and social - have crumbled , and been displaced by a sick , wild and impotent eclecticism `` Satyricon is a unique film in the way that it explores antiquity and evaluates the values of that and the society of 1969 . Fellini shows how we can not expect to fully understand the past , and to appreciate how we put our own values and beliefs onto the cultures that we look at . Although I do not feel antiquity was as absurd as the dream like state encapsulated by Fellini , I think he has allowed us to question the elements we are taught and given us the means to try and reconstruct ancient Rome in a different way . He allows us to accept that we will never have all the answers and even if we did , we may not understand all aspects of the society .
