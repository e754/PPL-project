Sodium chloride content in foods can be determined by titrating the chloride ion with silver , and excess silver ions with potassium chromate . The end point occurs when all the chloride ions are complexed and the excess silver ions react with the chromate ion to form the orange color of silver chromate . The endpoint in this reaction is therefore at the first hint of an orange color .
