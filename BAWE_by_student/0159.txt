Product development processes are a series of activities which companies conceive , design , manufacturing and sale a product for business profit . Part 2 introduced three main kinds of product development processes and analyzed their similar and different features . Companies make profits by finding customers ' need and creating products with low price and high quality to satisfy the need . Achieving these targets is not just one department 's responsibility , such as marketing , design and manufacturing . It is a whole product development process which involves all functions in a company . -LRB- Ulrich and Eppinger , 1995 -RRB- defined product development as `` a continuous activities start from forecasting and perception of potential marketing opportunities and finish with production , sales , delivery and after sale guaranty service . '' A product development process is a series of activities which companies conceive , design , manufacturing and sale a product for business profit . The processes are different between organizations , even different in diverse projects in an organization . Nevertheless , there is a generic product development process which has input of mission and output of product launch : The five phases which consist of generic product development process are concept development , system level design , detail design , testing and refinement , and product ramp-up . At first step in concept development phase , the target market need has been identified . The product 's concept which includes shape , function , features and specifications has been chosen for further steps . The system level design focus on the product system specifications like assembly layout . Then detail design includes whole product specifications of materials , components , and standard parts from suppliers . After that , testing and refinement is about test and evaluation all preproduction aspects of product . Finally , production ramp-up phase is for training the workforce and find any existing flaws in the whole process . This phase is often a continuous step . In fact , there are many different models used in product development processes . All of them have advantages and disadvantages and are applied to different industries . This part will identify three main models : First generation process , Stage gate process and Third generation process . National Aeronautics and Space Administration -LRB- NASA -RRB- introduced the first generation product development process called Phased Project Planning in 1960s . It often has exhaustive plan of working with suppliers and subcontractors and are applied in aerospace and military industrial projects . -LRB- Cooper , 1994 -RRB- It usually mentioned as Phased Review Process because of its specification . PRP divided whole product development process into separate phases with review points . Every phase work should be done under the condition that the previous phase has been finished successful . The resources are also allocated under the rules . It assures that the project is carried out in conceived way . Meantime , it decreases technical risk . But it has more disadvantage than advantages . First of all , the process is complex because of the every inspection for each phase . Then it is time-consuming because that the management need time to check each review point . Foremost , it is always executed by engineering which causes barrier of communication with other functions . Based on the above features , it is suitable for aerospace industry to avoid technical risk rather than business risk . It ca n't be applied for the condition that market changes frequently . -LRB- Wheelwright and Clark , 1992 -RRB- gave an example for this kind of functional based company called Kodak . Kodak 's main project management process is referred to Manufacturability Assurance Process . It is functional based in project leadership and adopt a series of phases which are focused and operated in functions . This kind of process with apparent function orientation is suitable for companies that insist on technical depth and technical solution is the competitive advantage . During several decades ' development and revolution , it is well know that multifunctional project team is effective and efficient for successful product development . Concurrent engineering is defined as a model which every function in company participate in the project team . -LRB- Cordero , 1991 -RRB- The function delegates communicate and integrate well in the project and have the responsibility and control of the development process with wider targets and ambitions . Multi-disciplinal team members use their professional skills together and share information with others . This situation leads the team to know every aspect of whole process earlier and comprehensive than before . For example , market opportunities , design process , financial investment and manufacturing capacities and so on . Meanwhile , the team staffs have close contact with all kinds of business participants , such as customers , competitors , and suppliers for earlier preparation of new product development . The crucial point for execution of this process is to trade off the team members ' professional skills and personalities . They need familiar with their own function skills and good teamwork spirit to cooperate with other functional staffs . The demerits are that the risk will increase by the concurrent simulation of design and manufacturing . In addition , the responsibility is easy to be shifted and transformed . Based on these features , the concurrent engineering process is suitable for increased production development which is in middle level of innovations , such as software , computer industries . Stage gate process is popular in business organizations , which is a combination model of conceptual and operational perspectives for efficient product development . -LRB- Cooper , 1990 -RRB- The basic structure of this process model is as following : The whole product development process is separate as sub processes called stages which process management skills can be used to . Gates means check points at the end of each sub process for consistent with whole process . Stages usually consist of similar or relative groups and activities and more costly than previous ones . Meanwhile , risk can be managed continuously by information purification at each stage . The gates have two functions : one is for the entrance for the next stage . The other is for control after previous stage . They bring input or output as features for different functions . For instance , the input maybe commutative items which project manager should bring to the gates or rules for evaluating project . They are different for vary situation and requirement . The output should be decisions for dealing with this stage , and admission for next stage . The gates are always kept by senior managers from different functions . Organization should change in two approaches for this process . One is that the project team are consist of multi-functional staffs and requires project manager understand every input and output of gates in the process . The other is that top management act as gate keeper . It leads to participation and commitment from them for product development . This process is advance in many aspects . For instance , it increase communication between different functions and enhance teamwork spirit . Meanwhile , it can reduce recycle rate and discover failure as soon as possible . Furthermore , marketing schedule joins the development process at the beginning for better launch . However , the implementation of this process is tough and relative difficult . Project progress is limited by each gate and it is impossible to overlap stages . It is time consuming for going through all stages and gates . None project is priority or more important than others which cause no connection with resource distribution . Motorola adopted this kind of process to build contract driven and cross-functional teams . -LRB- VVheelwright and Clark , 1992 -RRB- illustrated that the team is selected and execute activities under the guidance of senior management , who is act as project leader . At the beginning of the project , the team signs a contract with detail work plan which includes resource demand and expect operations results . In case the senior management approval , the contract is admitted both by key team members and senior managers . Leadership at this kind of process play more important and stronger role than other processes . Generally , these product development processes last eighteen to thirty months . Motorola 's framework process emphasis on integration and is suitable for the situation where system solution is the main factor . In this environment , this process is applied for platform projects which product innovation is the central of company . Meanwhile , it is efficient applied when the market atmosphere changes dramatically and sensitive responses are needed . In the above section , three models of product development process have been identified and introduced . First generation process , Concurrent engineering , and Stage gate process are all used for new product development . All of them are not perfect and have their advantages and disadvantages . They have their own unique features to apply in different situation and business . Nevertheless , as models of product development process , they have several similar issues as following . First of all , all of these three processes are customers ' orientation and begin with the perception of potential market opportunities and customer needs . Then they are separated into individual phases or stages from whole process . They all target at developing good quality product and assure outcomes ' quality by some checkpoint . For instance , first generation process has review points behind each phases and stage gate process has constraint gate with each stage . Meanwhile , all of them have testing or prototype steps to ensure final product quality . -LRB- Ulrich and Eppinger , 1995 -RRB- concluded that coordination is also a common element of these processes . They act as comprehensive plan which define every participator 's role and responsibilities . Cooperation and teamwork need to be implemented during the project . All of them need to be planned and schemed before start . Landmark timing of each phases or stages should be designed in the schedule . Foremost , good management is the common successful precondition for three processes . Outstanding management skills and open viewpoint and communication skills is essential for every process whether the management from functional managers or senior managers of organizations . Finally , all of the processes give the companies opportunities to improve existing systems and procedures . Although the mentioned three processes have similarities , they have their own unique characteristics for different applications . Concurrent engineering is more different from stage gate process than first generation process for project time and execution sequences . It has several multi-functional teams to effort in the same time . None stages or phases depend on the previous ones . This process causes that concurrent engineering process use shortest time to develop products than two others . Compare to the simultaneity activities of concurrent engineering processes , first generation process and stage gate process have strict sequences of each steps . Project maybe be delayed or hold by one stage 's problem . On the contrary , concurrent engineering process maybe departure its original schedule because of none gates or review points in it . It may increases risk for low quality or responsibility transformation . At team ingredient aspect , first generation process is functional orientation and the other two are multi-functional teams . First generation process is lack of communication by the functional orientation team structure . Meanwhile , the review point often checked by functional managers while stage gate process 's gate keepers are always senior managers . Stage gate process has the highest control level because of the input and output constraints of gates . The three processes are applied in different industries and situations . First generation process is often applied in aerospace or military industries because of its low technical risk and slow response for market changing . Concurrent engineering process is more appreciate for increasing product innovation and process innovations . Finally , stage gate process is used in most of business environment and efficient for fast market transformation situation .
