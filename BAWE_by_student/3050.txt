Information is the life-blood of tourism and so technology is fundamental to the ability of the industry to operate -LRB- Cooper , 2005 -RRB- . The surge of Internet use and availability has been phenomenal in recent years and has substantially affected virtually all sectors of tourism industry -LRB- Christou & Kassianidis 2002 -RRB- . Before exploring effects of new electronic distribution channels like internet on different players in marketplace , it is of great importance to take a look at how traditional electronic distribution channels work . A distribution channel is `` any organized and serviced system , paid for out of marketing budgets and created or utilized to provide convenient points of sales and / or access to consumers , away from the location of production and consumption '' -LRB- Middleton ,2001:292 -RRB- . The traditional distribution channels in tourism normally include the direct one in which principals , such as airlines , hotels and attractions directly sell their products and services to consumers and the indirect one in which intermediaries are used to provide travel information and sell travel products to consumers -LRB- O'Connor , 1999 -RRB- . Middleton -LRB- 2001 -RRB- defined intermediaries as `` any cooperate organization between principal and final consumer that facilitates purchases , the transfer of title to the buyer and sales revenue to the principal '' -LRB- Middleton , 2001:293 -RRB- . Specifically the travel distribution role has been performed by three intermediaries , travel agencies -LRB- retailers -RRB- , tour operators -LRB- wholesalers -RRB- and travel agencies -LRB- retailers -RRB- based in destination -LRB- Buhalis & Laws , 2001 -RRB- . Tour operators -LRB- aggregator -RRB- are intermediaries between suppliers and either travel agents or consumers . They purchase products and services from travel suppliers and package them into a product that they market to customer . -LRB- Inkpen , 1998 -RRB- . In this way aggregator builds relationship with customer and offers opportunity to purchase value package holiday -LRB- Buhalis , 2003 -RRB- . Travel agencies -LRB- integrator -RRB- , on the other hand , are intermediaries between customer and they sell supplier 's products and services to their customers and derive a commission for doing so -LRB- Inkpen , 1998 -RRB- . The traditional distribution channels were supported by a number of traditional electronic intermediaries -LRB- or traditional eTourism intermediaries -RRB- such as computer reservation systems -LRB- CRS -RRB- and global distribution systems -LRB- GDS -RRB- -LRB- Buhalis , 2003 -RRB- . Buhalis -LRB- 2003 -RRB- also introduced CRS and GDS as computerized systems that assist tourism enterprises with handling their inventories profitably and with facilitating the tourism products distribution . The appearance of Internet has become a new sales and marketing distribution channel for tourism business and therefore its rapid development had a profound impact on both principals and travel intermediaries -LRB- Cooper , 2005 ; Buhalis , 2001 ; O'Conner , 1999 ; Inkpen , 1998 -RRB- . Tourism suppliers -LRB- particularly airline , car rental and hotel chains -RRB- took advantage of the new opportunities and developed their own websites which allows users to access directly their reservation systems . -LRB- Buhalis , 2003 ; Buhalis ,2001 ; Sheldon , 1997 -RRB- . The advantages for principles are great because internet , as a direct channel , can not only save labor cost but also save distribution cost by eliminating travel agent commission . -LRB- Sheldon , 1997 ; Buhalis , 2001 , Buhalis , 2003 , Cooper , 2005 -RRB- . With the development of internet , a number of web-based travel agencies also emerged -LRB- e.g. URL -RRB- while off-line agencies developed their on-line provision -LRB- e.g. URL -RRB- . From the perspective of intermediaries , the rapid development of Internet offer both opportunities and threats to their existence -LRB- Sheldon , 1997 ; Buhalis , 2003 ; Cooper , 2005 -RRB- . On the one hand , most intermediaries actors realized the importance of using internet as a new media and quickly exploited the opportunities offered by internet and developed their online presence . Therefore they could quickly build complicated itineraries on line in accordance with specific need of customers by accessing into tourism suppliers ' database , finding up-to-date schedules , prices and availability data . -LRB- inkpen , 1998 -RRB- . In addition to that , Sheldon -LRB- 1997 -RRB- proposed that the internet may provide travel agents with a business opportunity by enabling them to promote their products to a broader geographic market at a low cost . On the other hand , consumer access to travel database creates an inevitable threat to the existence of travel intermediaries as consumers can plan their trip and purchase online . -LRB- O'Conner , 1999 -RRB- Moreover , intermediaries represents a considerable part in distribution cost and therefore it is understandable that the more players getting involved in a distribution channel , the more commissions and fees need to be paid and consequently increasing the price of the final product -LRB- Buhalis , 2003 -RRB- . So the direct and intermediate contact between suppliers and customers can reduce the importance of travel agencies as middleman in the customer-agent-supplier network and consequently result in the disintermediation . -LRB- Buhalis , 1998 -RRB- . However , Buhalis -LRB- 2003 -RRB- argued the travel intermediaries can remain their competiveness by enhancing their ability to `` re-engineer '' their core product and develop value-added services or final products . If intermediaries fail to do so , they will gradually loose their market share as more consumers will be purchasing tourism products directly from suppliers . So they need to identify their specific market segments and adopts different marketing strategy . From perspective of customers , the internet provides access to transparent and comparable information on destinations , holiday packages , travel and lodging services as well as providing prices and availabilities . -LRB- Buhalis , 2003 -RRB- . Apart from that , development of Internet created experienced and knowledgeable `` new '' tourists seeking exceptional value for time and money -LRB- Cooper , 2005 -RRB- . Moutinho -LRB- 1987 -RRB- referred the consumer behavior as `` the process of acquiring and organizing information in the direction of a purchase decision and of using and evaluating products and services '' -LRB- Moutinho ,1987 : 5 -RRB- .
