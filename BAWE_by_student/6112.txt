The human estrogen receptor is part of the Nuclear Receptor super family . The characteristic mechanism of these intracellular receptors is to bind their ligand , forming a ligand-receptor complex , which then acts as a transcription factor altering gene expression appropriately . The estrogen receptor -LRB- ERα -RRB- is located in the nucleus , in close proximity to the site of DNA action . The estrogen molecule , being small and lipophilic , is able to diffuse in to the cell and bind to the estrogen receptor . The binding of estrogen to ERα induces the dissociation of the multiple chaperone proteins that are associated with the inactivated ERα . -LRB- The function of the chaperone proteins is to ensure the unbound ER does not function without estrogen activation . The ERα then changes conformation which allows it to dimerise with another activated ERα , it can then bind to specific sites on the DNA -LRB- 2 -RRB- . The binding of the ERα dimer to the DNA is specific , it binds to estrogen response elements -LRB- EREs -RRB- which are specific palindromic sequences in the DNA . The DNA bound ERα dimer recruits transcription factor proteins -LRB- cofactors -RRB- that positively regulate gene expression , these proteins are called co-activators -LRB- 3 -RRB- . These characteristics ; of ligand binding , receptor dimerisation and DNA binding are functions of the protein that are made possible through its specific structure . The human estrogen receptor is only found in certain cells , activation of this receptor leads to the production of proteins involved in many different processes in both males and females . In women the processes include ovulation , implantation , pregnancy maintenance , childbirth and lactation , and in men the ERα is required for normal testicular function . Breast cancer is the most common form of cancer found in women ; it is on the increase and is therefore a serious concern . The lifetime risk of developing breast cancer is 1:11 for women and 1:1000 for men . Tumors mainly develop in ductal endothelial cells -LRB- 80 % -RRB- but can also develop in lobular epithelium -LRB- 20 % -RRB- . But what causes breast cancer and what are the underlying molecular mechanisms that lead to a tumor ? Cancer is a collection of diseases with the common features of uncontrolled cell growth followed by metastasis to surrounding tissues . It is this metastasis of the cancer to sensitive areas , such as the lymph nodes , that makes breast cancer lethal , as a tumor in the breast is not lethal in itself . Other areas breast cancers can metastasise to include the bone marrow and locally to other areas of the breast . The formation of a cancer is not triggered by any single event ; cancers are the result of an accumulation of errors in vital regulatory pathways . -LRB- It is thought that between 4 and 7 pathways have to be disrupted for a cancer to develop in humans . -RRB- Gene changes play a major role in disruption of cell signaling pathways and fall into two groups ; inherited and sporadic . Inherited gene changes leading to breast cancer account for 5 to 10 % of all breast cancer cases . However , it is important to note that expressing an inherited defective gene will not cause a tumor to develop in itself , but people with these mutated genes will have an increased susceptibility to developing cancer in their breast tissue . The three main mutated genes that are inherited through the germline are BRCA 1 , BRCA 2 and p53 . They are all tumor suppressor genes . Mutated forms of the BRCA 1 gene -LRB- BReast CAncer 1 -RRB- are found in half of the familial breast cancer cases and 80 % of early onset breast cancer cases . Women who possess mutated alleles of BRCA 1 have a 60 % possibility of developing breast cancer , compared to just 2 % in women with wild-type BRCA 1 -LRB- 1 -RRB- . It is located in the `` q '' arm of chromosome 17 and the protein product is 1,863 amino acids in length . Under normal circumstance this protein that BRCA 1 codes for is an accessory protein for Rad51 -LRB- 2 -RRB- . Rad51 is one of seven homologes of the Rec A protein , its function , which is dependent on BRCA 1 , involves the repair of replication forks accidentally damaged during the S Phase of the cell cycle . The removal of the Rad51 protein results in cell death whereas the removal or disruption of BRCA 1 leads to the accumulation of DNA damage . There are 31 positions along the BRCA 1 gene that are known to have mutated -LRB- 3 -RRB- . It is clear to see that any of these mutations , which affect the efficiency of BRCA 1 , can lead to the development of breast cancer . Another important gene that is commonly mutated in breast cancer is BRCA 2 -LRB- found on the `` q '' arm of chromosome 13 -RRB- . This is another tumor suppressor gene the protein product of which functions in much the same way as BRCA 1 , ensuring the DNA repair protein Rad51 functions properly . Mutations in BRCA 2 account for almost as much inherited susceptibility to early onset breast cancer as mutations in BRCA 1 . A third gene involved in breast cancer is the p53 gene . This is another tumor suppressor gene and is found to be mutated in at least 50 % of all cancer cases . It is found in all cells in the body and has 3 main roles ; controlling the cell cycle , triggering apoptosis and ensuring genetic stability . A cell with either a mutated form of p53 , or with the p53 protein absent altogether , will grow uncontrollably . Wild type p53 is activated when there is damage to the DNA of a cell . The effect p53 has on the cell depends on its current position in the cell cycle . If the DNA damage is detected during the G1 phase then p53 prevents further progress until the DNA has been repaired . If the DNA damage is detected during the G2 phase then p53 triggers apoptosis -LRB- programmed cell death -RRB- -LRB- 4 -RRB- . P53 exert this effect on the cell cycle in an indirect way . It first binds to damaged DNA , through a non-sequence specific binding site , which activates the p53 . A second binding domain on the protein then binds to a pallindromic 10 base pair sequence on the DNA that activates either cell cycle inhibitors or repair proteins . Two important genes that are regulated by p53 in this way -LRB- i.e. who have this 10 base pair sequence in their promoter region -RRB- are p21 and GADD45 . p21 is a cdk protein -LRB- cyclin dependent kinase -RRB- that inhibits the cell cycle and GADD45 is a DNA repair protein -LRB- 4 -RRB- . It is easy to see , therefore , that loss of p53 function in a cell gives it a growth advantage over other cells . Mutations in p53 , as well as the BRCA genes , encourage cancers by inhibiting DNA repair and by allowing cells with mutated DNA to proliferate . These are major problems but , as has been mentioned , will not alone cause a cancer to grow . People without these genetic mutations will be just as vulnerable to carcinogens and mutagens as people with these mutations , but will be far more able to repair the damage these carcinogens are capable of doing , and therefore be at much less risk of developing breast cancer . The oestrogen receptor plays a major role in breast cancer as most breast cancers are oestrogen dependent -LRB- ER + -RRB- . This means that the cancerous cells rely on oestrogen to grow and proliferate . Therefore changes in the oestrogen receptor -LRB- ER -RRB- , and chemicals that mimic oestrogen -LRB- oestrogenic chemicals -RRB- , are a concern . Due to the majority of breast tumors being ER + most of the treatments available aim to either block the oestrogen receptor or to reduce the concentration of oestrogen in or around the cancerous cells . A group of drugs used , called anti-oestrogens , act as oestrogen antagonists , and include drugs such as Tamoxifen and Raloxifen -LRB- 7 -RRB- . A group of drugs that are widely used are the aromatase inhibitors -LRB- AIs -RRB- . These drugs aim to reduce the concentration of oestrogen in cancerous cells . AIs are traditionally used to treat breast cancer in premenopausal women , as oestrogen activity is restricted to the breast and ovaries during this time , but trials are currently underway to investigate the effectiveness of using AIs on postmenopausal women . AIs are not prescribed to postmenopausal women due to the wide distribution of oestrogen throughout the body during this time . Herceptin , a relatively new anti-cancer drug , works by binding to a growth factor receptor , reducing the stimulus for the cancer cells to proliferate -LRB- 8 -RRB- . These treatments are effective , but only if the tumor remains oestrogen dependent . Most breast tumors develop oestrogen resistance though , making endocrine treatment useless and therefore relying on the more traditional methods , such as radiotherapy and possibly surgery . So it can be seen that for a tumor to develop in breast tissue there has to be a series of errors , involving damage to DNA , damage to genes controlling aspects of the cell -LRB- such as DNA repair and replication -RRB- and an increased stimulus for cells to proliferate . These errors can culminate in a tumor that has the ability to metastasise to other areas of the body with possibly lethal consequences . Therefore , oestrogenic chemicals in our environment , which have the potential to encourage the growth of breast tumors , need to be identified and assessed for the potential threat they present .

Sonic Hedgehog -LRB- Shh -RRB- is a secreted signalling molecule integral to the patterning of the early vertebrate embryo . Shh is a mammalian homologue of Hh -LRB- Hedgehog -RRB- , a signalling molecule present in Drosophila Melanogaster -LRB- invertebrate -RRB- , where it is also seen to play a vital role in embryogenesis . Other mammalian Hh homologues include Indian Hedgehog -LRB- Ihh -RRB- and Desert Hedgehog -LRB- Dhh -RRB- . The fact that the Shh molecule itself , and the functional pathway through which Shh operates , are both seen to be well conserved throughout vertebrates and invertebrates , suggests that this signalling molecule is of the utmost importance to the correct patterning of embryos during development . Much of the functional information available for Shh has been elucidated from work on D. Melanogaster , fortunately , a lot of this information is applicable and relevant to vertebrates , as a result , much is known about the distribution and function of Shh within mammals . Shh function can be divided into two areas ; specification of neuronal progenitor cells and control of proliferation and survival of neural cells . The role of Shh in progenitor cell specification -LRB- and therefore the main role of Shh in development -RRB- will be the main focus here . Shh directs the morphology and organisation of the developing embryo by ensuring the correct fate of neural progenitor cells -LRB- undifferentiated cells that will develop into highly specialised cells of the central nervous system -LRB- CNS -RRB- , such as motor neurons , interneurons or glial cells -RRB- . Shh is seen to play a major role in the dorsoventral patterning of the neural tube -LRB- Echelard et al. , 1993 ; Ho and Scott , 2002 -RRB- , oligodendrocyte specification , eye patterning -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- , anterior-posterior patterning of the limbs -LRB- Riddle et al. , 1993 ; Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- and induction of the ventral somites -LRB- the longitudinal segments of the vertebrate body -RRB- -LRB- Johnson et al. , 1994 ; Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- . Proliferative and survival roles of Shh include , neural stem cell proliferation , neuronal cell survival and glial cell survival -LRB- Ho and Scott , 2002 -RRB- . The basic mechanism by which Shh directs cell fate is common to many of these different functions , and as has been said , is also seen to be shared between the vertebrates and invertebrates . The mechanisms of Shh that are common to different functions will be discussed first , followed by a look at more specific mechanisms of Shh action , such as the specific way by which this signalling molecule directs dorsoventral patterning of the neural tube . As Shh is a secreted protein , a secretory cell is of course required . These secretory cells , specialised to produce , modify and release Shh appropriately , have been located in several areas of the developing embryo , the main regions being the floor plate of the neural tube and the notochord -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- . The neural tube is formed from ectodermal tissue -LRB- the outer layer of the three layers of germ cells of the developing embryo -RRB- and develops into the CNS -LRB- that is , the spinal cord and brain -RRB- . The floor plate of this neural tube is also ectodermal in origin and is derived from the neural plate , the neural plate being a ` plate ' of thickened ectodermal tissue located along the dorsal midline of the early embryo . The notochord is a longitudinal arrangement of cells that forms the supporting axis of the body in chordates -LRB- which includes vertebrates -RRB- . In the higher vertebrates , such as mammals , the notochord is obliterated by the vertebrae as they develop . The presence of Shh in these areas of the embryo clearly implicate it in playing an important role in the development of the embryo . Other areas in which Shh has been located include the early gut endoderm -LRB- the inner most of the three layers of germ cells of the embryo -RRB- , oligodendrocytes -LRB- a type of glial cell -RRB- , the limb buds and the Hensen node -LRB- a feature found in bird embryos -RRB- -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- . The Shh signalling molecule is the result of several post translational modifications of a larger precursor protein . After signal sequence cleavage following translation , the Shh precursor is approximately 45kDa in size , consisting of 462 amino acids -LRB- human homologue -RRB- -LRB- Marigo et al. , 1995 -RRB- . This precursor then undergoes autocatalytic cleavage , which involves the molecule catalysing the breakage of a covalent bond within its own primary structure . The resulting fragments include a 25kDa C terminal molecule and a 20 kDa N terminal molecule -LRB- Porter et al. , 1996 -RRB- . -LRB- The size of the N terminal molecule has been reported to be 20kDa -LRB- Porter et al. , 1996 -RRB- , 19kDa -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- and 22kDa -LRB- Ho and Scott , 2002 -RRB- -RRB- . The role of Shh signalling in dorsoventral patterning will now be considered , as an example of how the common pathway described above can be modified to produce a diverse set of results . In the early embryo , neuronal progenitor cell fate needs to be carefully regulated in order to produce a fully functional CNS . Shh , secreted from the notochord and floor plate , regulates cell fate through a concentration gradient system . Progenitor cells of the neural tube detect the level of Shh and respond appropriately . Responses could involve differentiation of progenitor cells , either into motor neurons -LRB- efferent or effector neurons -RRB- or interneurons -LRB- connecting neurons -RRB- -LRB- Ericson et al. , 1996 ; Ho and Scott , 2002 -RRB- . The specific responses of neuronal progenitor cells to Shh levels are brought about by the regulation -LRB- activation or repression -RRB- of gene expression of a variety of homeobox containing transcription factors . A homeobox is a protein domain , consisting of approximately 60 residues , that regulates a certain gene by recognising and binding to a specific DNA sequence within the gene . Homeodomain proteins regulated by Shh signals include Pax 6 -LRB- a member of the paired-box family -RRB- , Irx 3 -LRB- a member of the Iroquois family -RRB- , Dbx 1 and Dbx2 -LRB- members of the DEAD box family -RRB- , which are grouped into Class I , as they share the common feature of being expressed in the absence of Shh . Class II homeodomain proteins include Nkx 2.2 and Nkx 6.1 , which are expressed in the presence of Shh signals -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- . The combination of expression of these various transcription factors defines five distinct regions of the ventral neural tube in which specific cell types are produced . These regions remain distinct through the mutual repression observed between transcription factors in neighbouring regions . By establishing these distinct domains , Shh signals are responsible for playing a major role in organising the early patterning of the ventral CNS . The role of Shh described above is the best characterised role for Shh in the CNS , and possibly the most important . However , Shh is involved in other developmental processes , such as the late development of the dorsal CNS , -LRB- although the exact role it plays here is less clear -RRB- . In the dorsal region , Shh is considered to play a role in inducing proliferation of progenitor granule cells , and so here , Shh is undertaking a different role to that of directing ventral patterning , as in this case , Shh is acting as a mitogen and not as a director of cell fate -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- . Shh is also known to play a role in determining oligodendrocyte cell fate . Oligodendrocyte precursor cells require Shh signals in order to differentiate and these precursors are seen to only originate from areas of CNS that involve Shh signalling . The fact that oligodendrocyte differentiation occurs at a later stage of embryogenesis and requires similar levels Shh to induce this differentiation , suggests that oligodendrocytes and differentiated ventral neurons share a common precursor . A role for Shh in observed in the eye , where Shh is considered to be involved in the development of the optic cup , regulation of proliferation and differentiation of retinal precursor cells and some aspects of cell movement , such as growth cone control -LRB- control of the motile tips of growing neurons -RRB- and neural crest cell migration -LRB- Marti and Bovolenta , 2002 -RRB- . Shh is also required to ensure cell survival of neuronal progenitor cells -LRB- neuroepithelial cells -RRB- during early development . PTC induces apoptotic cell death in the absence of Shh signals , so in the early embryo Shh is required for the control of cell fate and to block the pro-apoptotic signal conveyed by PTC into the cells -LRB- Thibert et al. , 2003 -RRB- . So it can be seen that as well as playing a major role in patterning the ventral CNS during early embryogenesis , Shh signalling is also needed for aspects of later embryogenesis , -LRB- such as neuroepithelial cell survival and dorsal CNS patterning -RRB- , organogenesis -LRB- such as the eye -RRB- and is also involved in oligodendrocyte cell differentiation . The role of Shh in these integral aspects of embryogenesis , as well as the distinct concentration gradient mechanism by which Shh signals , would justify describing Sonic hedgehog as the archetype of developmental signalling molecules .
