This report is concerned with the design of discrete controllers , and the comparison of theoretical to simulated controllers . A basic First Order Plus Dead Time -LRB- FOPDT -RRB- system was selected to be controlled and various preliminary calculations were carried out to determine suitable values for system controller coefficients .
