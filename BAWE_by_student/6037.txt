The aim of the experiment was to use the Wheatstone bridge to determine the resistance of a semi-conductor at various temperatures . The method involved setting up the Wheatstone bridge box , with a galvanometer attached to it . A beaker that was full of water had a semi-conductor inserted into it so different temperatures of water could be taken in order to measure resistance at various temperatures . The temperature of the water was taken approximately every 10C . At each temperature reading the resistance was found by determining the value of the adjustable resistance that gave no deflection on the galvanometer when the key was depressed . When the resistance was greater than 1000 ohms the multiplier had to be used . From the results obtained an ordinary and log graph were drawn to shows trends in the data . The main trend was as the temperature increased the resistance of the semi-conductor decreased . This trend was expected because increasing the temperature meant that electrons were promoted to the conduction band . The graph of the logarithm of the resistance plotted against the temperature showed a linear relationship . There were no major anomalous results as the points plotted fitted the linear relationship . However one of the results showed a large decrease in resistance of the semi-conductor between 0C and 16.0 C . This may have been due to the change in state of the water from a solid to a liquid . Per 10C increase the resistance decreased by a factor of approximately 1.5 . To conclude , as the temperature increased the resistance of a semi-conductor decreased . This was because resistivity is related to the behaviour of electrons in the material and the temperature of the semi-conductor . When the temperature was increased , it allowed electrons to be freed from their atomic orbits . This meant that more electrons were in the conduction band because they were promoted from the valence bad so the resistance was lowered . Small sources of error in the experiment were in the temperature readings and resistance measurements . It was very hard to know the exact temperature of the water when the resistance was being measured as it was constantly cooling down . When the resistance was being measured it was extremely difficult to prevent no current passing through the galvanometer but only a minimal amount was allowed through . An improvement to the experiment which would make it more accurate and confirm the trend would be to take more readings and repeat readings so the linear relationship could be confirmed . An application of resistance in food systems is in determining the quality of food . It can be used to detect bruising in fruit tissue . For a metal conductor , an increase in temperature causes an increase in resistance . The opposite is true for a semi - conductor because as temperature increases , resistance decreases . A metal conductor has a lattice structure and when the temperature increases , it increases the atom vibrations in the lattice structure . This causes the collision frequency between the charge carriers and the lattice structure to increase which in turn causes a reduction in speed of the charge carriers . Therefore the resistance is increased . With a semi-conductor a temperature increase causes vibration of atoms that break some of the bonds between the valence electrons and the lattice atoms . There are lots of concerns over the use of genetically modified organisms in the production of food . This topic is incredibly controversial and appears very frequently in the news . Naturally , consumers are wary about new technologies as no one can predict the long term effects of genetically modified food , even with detailed scientific research and trials . Most consumers rely on the media to make their judgment on genetically modified food . These sources do provide information but some tend to be bias and simplified . This may mean consumers lose the right to an informed decision due to the lack of information available to them . The consumer concerns will be considered to see if they are justified but also the benefits that genetically modified food may hold for the world will be examined . Also the background of genetically modified organisms and how they are created will be explored . Genetically modified is a term used to describe the insertion , enhancement or deletion of a particular characteristics of an organism . This is done by altering genes in the DNA of a specific organism . The insertion of specific genes may either be from a species very closely related to itself or from a distant species . This is because all genes come from DNA and all DNA has the same basic structure . Genetic modification involves removing a desired gene from one organism and joining the gene onto a plasmid . The plasmid and desired gene together are known as recombinant DNA . The recombinant DNA is then inserted into a bacterium , in which reproduction occurs . This process produces more bacterium with copies of recombinant DNA . The bacterium , with the copies of recombinant DNA then infects the organism that requires the specific characteristic . Before genetic modification , desired traits were achieved through farmers by selective breeding . However this method is very time consuming and it is not accurate unlike genetic modification . Genetic modification is more specific and can create the exact characteristic . There are many consumer concerns about the use of genetically modified food . These concerns can be categorised into environmental hazards , health risks and economic concerns . All these categories involve whether genetically modified food is safe and will be examined closely . Environmental hazards of genetic engineering include inadvertent gene transfer to non target species . For example , crops that have been engineered to be herbicide tolerant might crossbreed with weeds creating ` super weeds ' , that would be herbicide tolerant . This is a very justified concern because if ` super weeds ' were to be introduced into the environment it may also affect the growth of other crops that are essential food for humans . There is also concern that genetically modified crops could reduce the effectiveness of pesticides . Many people worry that insects will become resistant to crops that have been genetically modified to produce their own pesticides , therefore reducing the quantity of food . Genetic modification may also lead to undesirable consequences such as reducing the biodiversity of traditional crops . The health risks involved with genetically modified food include the tendency to promote allergic reactions and the unknown effects on health . As allergies increase in society it is important to minimise the risk of allergic reactions to genetically modified food . This can be done by not transferring genes from species where there is a known allergic reaction such as peanuts but genetic modification may also introduce new allergies into society . Extensive testing of genetically modified food may be required to avoid the possibility of harm to consumers with food allergies . As the effects on human health have only been researched over a small time scale , the long term risk to human health is unknown . Introducing new genes into food may have a negative effect on human health that may not be noticeable for years . Another concern relates to the vectors used for transforming plant cells . These contain genes for antibiotic resistance . These antibiotic resistant genes enter the transformed plant with the desired gene . The antibiotic resistant gene may be passed into the E.coli bacteria in the gut , making it resistant to some types of antibiotics . This concern is antibiotics are very commonly used in medical treatments . The consumer concern over the economics of genetically modified food is justified with two strong arguments . One is the cost marketing the genetically modified food . It is an expensive process and consumers worry that biotech companies just want to make a profit and are not interested in consumer welfare . The other concern involves the patents on the genetically modified crops . The concern is that the developing countries farmers will not be able to afford the seeds for the genetically modified crop . This is a big problem because one of the benefits of genetically modified food is it will ensure that there is enough food for the world 's population , especially in the developing countries . Some of the consumer concerns are justified but genetically modified food may hold many benefits . The main advantage is ensuring an adequate food supply . The world 's population is increasing and one of the ways to meet the demand is through the genetic manipulation of plants . There are many ways that genetic modification can increase the amount of food produced and make crops more useful . These include , enabling plants to become cold or drought resistant , improving resistance to pests , improving storage time and enhancing the production of vitamins . Cold and drought resistant plants are important because when there are extreme climate changes crops can fail . Unexpected cold weather can destroy seedlings . If the crop is genetically modified using an antifreeze gene the seedlings will not die , therefore ensuring a food supply . As insect pests reduce the world 's crop population by over 13 % per year it is important to combat pests . This can be done by the use of chemicals . However this method causes environmental problems as the run off of agricultural wastes from excessive pesticides can contaminate the water supply and the pesticides could be damaging to consumers health . By genetic modification of pest resistant plants , less pesticides need to be used on plants . An example of a pest resistant plant is maize as it contains a gene from the bacterium Bacillus thuringiensis -LRB- Bt gene -RRB- . It is the protein that is produced by the Bt gene that is toxic to some insect pests . The insect resistant genetically modified maize is rarely sprayed with pesticides because the Bt gene provides continuous protection to the plant because of the toxic protein that is being produced . Another consumer advantage of the genetically modified maize is that the insecticide is confined just to the maize so there is less damage to the surrounding environment . Improving storage time is important , so consumers get a product that keeps longer and fewer crops are wasted . Genetic modification for storage is shown in the FlavrSavr tomato . Usually when tomatoes ripen a gene is triggered and a chemical is released that causes the fruit to soften and then rot . Scientists have modified that gene in the tomato by switching it off , so the tomato softens slowly and this prolongs shelf life . In developing countries malnutrition is common because people are unable to have a varied diet . Genetically modifying a crop like rice , so it contains added vitamins and minerals , not usually found in rice could be beneficial . It may help combat some of the vitamin deficiency and improve the health of people . In conclusion , some consumer concerns are justified however genetically modified food does also have some benefits . The main justified concern is the unknown effect on human health . The short and long term effects of genetically modified food need to be researched further and this will allow consumers to make a more informed decision about genetically modified food . At present some consumer concerns are due to lack of information about genetically modified organisms and do n't understand all the implications involved with genetically modified food . The government understands consumer concerns and takes the human right of adequate food supply and has tried to alleviate some of the their concerns with labelling laws and policies . The policies state that all genetically modified foods exported must be labelled . This allows consumers to have a choice whether they take the risk and buy genetically modified product or not . However consumers must also realise the potential of genetically modified food in the future . It is a way of ensuring a food supply for the world , with benefits such as pest resistant crops and increase nutritional value of crops . This powerful technology has got to be applied with caution if it is going to be implemented successfully on a world scale . Genetic modification may also need to be refined slightly to improve safety and reassure consumers . However scientists must always remember that once food has been genetically altered there is no way of getting back to the food original makeup .

Dietary antioxidant found in fruit and vegetables include vitamin C , carotenoids , Vitamin E , flavonoids and polyphenols . -LRB- Geissler and Powers , 2005 -RRB- An important reaction took place in order to determine the antioxidant potential of the samples . The ferric-tripyridyltriazine complex -LRB- Fe 3 + - TPTZ -RRB- was reduced by the ferrous -LRB- Fe 2 + -RRB- , formed by the antioxidant and this produced a blue colour so the absorbance could be measured . Grapefruit had the lowest absorbance value of 0.9105 so had less antioxidant capacity . Various teas and coffees tested had larger absorbance and FRAP values and therefore a greater antioxidant capacity than the fruit juices tested . Figure 1 showed positive correlation between the concentration of vitamin C and absorbance value . This correlation suggested that the higher the concentration of Vitamin C , the greater the antioxidant potential . However vitamin C 1500 µM concentration had a much higher absorbance value than expected at 1.4293 and was higher than vitamin C 2000 µM concentration and this trend should not have occurred . The FRAP values had a wide range from vitamin C 100 µM with a value of 327.29 µM , through to green tea 2707.14 µM . From the protein composition of food , protein intake can be determined using protein assays . The Bradford method has a number of advantages over the Lowry method including the sensitivity , greater speed -LRB- × 10 -RRB- , lower susceptibility to interference and only requires one reagent . This study compared the total protein content of cows versus soya milk and determined the protein concentration of a bovine serum albumin -LRB- BSA -RRB- standard of unknown concentration . The milk samples and unknown solution had similar protein content when determined using the same method . Protein content determination of the samples using the Lowry method showed a higher protein content than the Bradford method and a higher value than expected . In the Lowry method the range of data for protein content was 5.76 - 6.79 g/100 ml compared to the Bradford method where protein content was lower and ranged from 2.55-3 .46 g/100 ml . The BSA standard curves for both methods had good positive correlation and excellent linearity . The Bradford method had a slightly higher R 2 value than Lowry method as the R 2 value for the Bradford method was 0.9861 compared to 0.9718 . The protein content determined for the unknown sample using the Lowry method was significantly higher at 6.79 g/100 ml than any of the other milk samples analysed using this method . Both of the standard curves showed that the absorbency readings at concentration of BSA at 0.7 mg/ml were slightly lower than expected as both these values lay below the line of best fit . The Lowry method gave protein content values higher than expected results for all the samples ranging from 5.46 - 6.79 g/100 ml . The reason for these higher values may have been due to inaccurate dilution of the milk samples . The dilution of milk in the Lowry method was higher than in the Bradford method and this may account for the slightly higher results if dilution was slightly inaccurate . In both methods the dilution factor had to be taken into consideration in the calculations . The unknown sample had two different protein contents according to the methods used . The Lowry method determined protein content for the unknown sample of 6.79 g/100 ml where as the Bradford method had a value of 2.55 g/100 ml . The unknown sample may have been semi-skimmed milk as the literature value for its protein content is 3.3 g/100 ml and this value lies between the experimental values . The lower than expected absorbency readings on the BSA standard curves at 0.7 mg/ml may have been due to inadequate measurement of the BSA or mixing of the samples . This could be improved by using a more accurate pipette and thorough mixing of the samples using a vortex . The reproducibility of the Bradford method data is achievable and should produce accurate results as the method is sensitive , fast , and easy and has proved to be the preferred method as results match literature values . The Lowry method is less suitable to reproduce data as it is time consuming and produces a slightly wider range of results . However the results obtained by the Lowry method were only approximately 3 g above the literature value and this method is good for similar applications . According to the nutritional information on the label of whole milk , there is 3.4 g/100 ml protein . The result obtained for whole milk from the Bradford method was very close to the literature value at 3.46 g / 100 ml . However the value obtained for the whole milk using the Lowry method was 5.76 g/100 ml , higher than the actual protein content . Protein content determination is important for both assessing the nutritional value of foodstuffs and estimating the population protein intake . Proteins are vital in the body as cells are composed of them and their metabolism regulated by them .
