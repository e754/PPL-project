`` Most of the matter in the universe , is dark , so what makes scientists think that it exists and what could it be ? '' The mass of a galaxy can be predicted from its size and the amount of light that it gives off , however , astronomers ' measurements about the mass of galaxies found by looking at their speed of rotation , show that the mass required in order for these galaxies to rotate at their current speed , would be ten to one hundred times more than we can see . Since this mass is not visible it is called dark matter . It is believed that `` stars make up less than 1 % of the universe 's mass ; all the loose gas and other forms of matter , less than 5 % '' -LRB- 1 -RRB- , and the rest is made up of dark matter and dark energy , 25 % of which is dark matter . However , nobody knows what dark matter actually is , even though there is much evidence for its existence . This is because it neither reacts with any known form of matter or emits any electromagnetic radiation , in fact the effect of dark matter can only be seen by its gravitational pull on visible matter . There are two different types of dark matter , hot dark matter and cold dark matter . Hot dark matter travels close to the speed of light and only makes 0.3 % of the total mass of the universe ; it is likely to be made up of neutrinos . Cold dark matter on the other hand is much more sluggish and makes up anything up to 25 % of the total mass of the universe , however nobody knows for certain what cold matter is made up of . In this report , I will be concentrating on cold dark matter I will be looking at the numerous types of evidence for the existence of cold dark matter and what it could be . The existence of dark matter was first considered with the discovery that the stars in the sky are accelerating at a greater rate than would be expected for the amount of gravitational pull that occurs from other stars . Therefore scientists deduced that there must be some kind of matter between the stars that does not react with anything or emit electromagnetic radiation , however does provide the required mass to cause the gravitational pull in order for the stars too travel at their rate of acceleration . It is this gravitational pull factor of the dark matter that provides much more of its sources of evidence . It was then realised that the expected mass of the universe was much less than was actually predicted and therefore the idea of dark matter really kicked off . At the beginning of the universe , before the period of accelerated expansion , the whole universe was uniform with no clusters of mass . However , after the period of accelerated expansion , local irregularities had occurred due to the expansion rate being greater than the speed of light , meaning that matter at one end of the universe would not be able to interact with matter at the other end in order to stay within equilibrium . However , at this time in the lifespan of the universe its temperature was still far too great for the matter to cluster together , according to the amount of mass from the matter in the universe . This is because the high amounts of energy would simply force any matter to annihilate each other , forming smaller matter that could not be clustered together . Therefore , dark matter is needed in order to provide the extra mass that could cluster all of these high energy particles together and stop them from escaping . It is believed that only cold dark matter could have caused this clustering as it is much more sluggish than hot dark matter , and would therefore be better able to cluster things together quickly . It is this cold dark matter that is still causing galaxies to cluster together today . The presence of dark matter stabilises the formed galaxies and clusters of galaxies as they continue to collide , merge , and evolve , as quoted by Kaufmann and Freedman in their fifth edition of the book Universe : `` The total mass of our galaxy is much more than the amount of visible mass . As for our galaxy , we conclude that ; it must contain significant amounts of non-luminous dark matter . This was discovered in the 1930s by two scientists named Zwicky and Smith , who looked at the individual galaxies making up two nearby galaxy clusters , called Coma and Virgo . They then calculated the velocity of these galaxies before deducing that the velocities were between a factor of ten and one hundred that of what they had expected . They knew that the only factor that causes a change in the velocity of a cluster of galaxies is the gravitational pull between all of the galaxies , and that the greater the velocity the greater the gravitational pull , and therefore mass must be . From this they realised that the clusters must have a much greater mass than expected and this must be made up from dark matter . Perhaps the strongest form of evidence for the existence of dark matter is the rotation curves of galaxies . Rotation curves of galaxies were first measured by people such as Rubin and Freeman in the 1970s . It had long been known that galaxies rotate around their centre much like the planets in our solar system and that they also obey Kepler 's laws . Therefore , finding the rotation velocity of galaxies can enable you to measure the mass within that galaxy to a high accuracy . On the outside of galaxies there is much less light , meaning that there is much less mass concentrated in these regions . This would mean that these areas should rotate at a much lower rate , however this is not the case . This indicates that there must be a greater mass within each individual galaxy than previously expected . This experiment has been carried on many spiral galaxies including our own , and is the first evidence that dark matter exists within each individual galaxy . Further evidence of dark matter existing within individual galaxies has been revealed from x-ray observations of galaxies . These x-ray images show that clusters are filled with a very hot , low density gas that would easily leak away if it was not held by some strong gravitational pull , like that provided by the existence of dark matter . This dark matter can be seen from gamma ray emissions from within the galaxies . Despite this resounding evidence for the existence of dark matter , some scientists are starting to believe that it may not actually exist , and that instead , a change in the laws of physics is needed . The reason for this theory is that as dark matter can not be seen or measured , nobody actually knows what it is made up of , although there have been many predictions . It is already known that hot dark matter is likely to be made up of particles that can travel close to the speed of light , such as a non-zero mass neutrinos . One thing that scientists are certain about is what dark matter can not be made up of . Firstly there is no way that dark matter can be made up of protons , neutrons , or anything that these join together to form , this is because at the beginning of the universe there simply was n't enough of these to produce the required quantities of dark matter . Neither can dark matter be simply hidden within vast numbers of black holes or neutron stars , as a black hole large enough to hold the required amount of dark matter would produce and emit numerous x-rays that are easy to detect . A small fraction of dark matter may be formed from neutrinos , however not a great amount , as they are probably too lightweight to cause things such as galaxy clusters . Scientists believe that the answer to dark matter belongs in an unknown `` exotic '' particle called a WIMP . WIMP stands for Weakly Interacting Massive Particles , and it is predicted that for the mass density of the universe to be critical the vast majority of dark matter must be made of these non-baryonic particles . WIMPs are in fact very strong and can therefore travel through things easily . It is predicted that millions could be passing through your body this very second . One of the most commonly recognised theories about WIMPs , is that of supersymmetry . Supersymmetry explains how all known elementary particles have a `` superpartner '' , which are much heavier and therefore more sluggish than the original particle . One such superpartner is the neutralino , the partner for a photon . The neutralino has a zero charge so is not affected by electromagnetic forces , and is also stable , meaning it wo n't decay into a smaller superparticle . From the big bang theory scientists can predict how many neutralinos were produced , and how many still existed after the beginning of the universe , due to annihilation during collisions . Based on the predicted number of neutralinos and their expected mass `` the total mass in neutralinos almost exactly matches the inferred mass of dark matter in the universe . This correspondence is a strong sign that neutralinos are indeed dark matter '' -LRB- 4 -RRB- . Other undetected particles that have been suggested to exist include axions . Another possibility is that dark galaxies may exist ; these galaxies are much smaller than our own and can not be detected , as they only include black and brown dwarfs . It is now the job of scientists to detect these possibilities of dark matter ; numerous experiments are now underway to do just this . Although neutralinos do not interact with matter , there are so many of them that occasionally a neutralino will hit an atomic nucleus . When this occurs , the kinetic energy from the neutralino is transferred into the nucleus in the form of heat energy , and it is this that needs to be measured . These experiments most commonly take place underground , using large water canisters ; this means that background cosmic rays which could affect the experiment are eliminated . The Bohr model of the atom has electrons placed around the nucleus much like planets orbiting the sun in the heliocentric solar system . However , these electrons can only exist on set energy levels and not between orbits . Despite the Bohr model having many flaws , it has been considered a success and is still taught despite being proved incorrect . This essay shall show some of the flaws of the model as well as explain how it has become a success despite or because of these flaws . Prior to the Rutherford-Bohr models of the atom , J J Thompson came up with the idea of the plum pudding atom . This consisted of electrons and protons placed randomly within the atom much like a plum pudding , they were kept in place by the equilibrium of the magnetic repulsions and attractions , between each electron and photon . However , this was discredited by Rutherford a few years later . Rutherford , along with Marsden and Geiger did an experiment , in which they fired high energy alpha particles at a sheet of gold foil . Rutherford placed a fluorescent screen around the foil that would detect the scattered alpha particles . As expected most particles simply travelled straight through the foil , however some were scattered at acute angles and a minute number even deflected straight back . This brought Rutherford to the conclusion , that the atom was mainly made up of nothing , but had an area of dense mass in the middle , now known as the nucleus . He also deduced that this area must be positively charged as it deflected the positively charged alpha particles . He was therefore able to come to the conclusion that the negatively charged electrons must orbit around this nucleus , hence discrediting Thompson 's model . Rutherford 's `` classical '' model of the atom , consisted of the positively charged nucleus in the middle , with the electrons placed on random orbits around the nucleus , much like in the heliocentric model of the solar system . However , the shortcomings of this model were obvious from the start , and were spotted by Danish physicist Niels Bohr , who was born in 1913 . Bohr realised that this classical model of an atom does not satisfy laws of quantum mechanics . If the electrons were to be spiralling around the nucleus , they would be accelerating centripetally , this would mean that the electrons would emit electro-magnetic waves , however this was not the case . More interesting , was that due to the acceleration the electrons would also lose energy and therefore velocity , this would mean the electrons would spiral in towards the centre of the atom , destroying it . Bohr came up with his own model of the atom , and it was the first which combines the quantum world of Planck and Einstein to the more classical model of the atom , as used by Rutherford . Bohr 's model of the atom had electrons still orbiting the nucleus , but instead of being placed randomly , they could only exist on certain energy levels . This means that each electron has a certain set energy and therefore does n't emit radiation making it spiral into the nucleus . `` The ` stable ' orbits of the electrons correspond to certain fixed amounts of energy , each a multiple of the basic quantum , but there were no in-between orbits because they would require fractional amounts of energy '' . -LRB- 1 -RRB- . In the Bohr model the electrons can be excited by the absorption of the light photon , which moves it up to a higher energy level . However , this makes the electron unstable due to a free space now in the lower level -LRB- the ground state -RRB- . Therefore the electron will fall back to the lower energy level , releasing energy in the form of a photon . The wavelength of this photon depends on the change in energy level which is different for each element . Bohr stated that two electrons could be present in the inner orbit , with eight needed on the rest , if this value is not eight then the atom will be unstable , looking for another atom to join up with . Despite this model by Bohr having obvious flaws , it has proved to be extremely successful . One reason for this is that it was the first model of the atom , which linked quantum ideas to the classical ideas of the atom , and in the process answered the problems of radiating energy and spiralling electrons . The biggest success of this model came with the simple hydrogen atom , which is the most elementary of particles with just electron orbiting the nucleus . The calculated spectral lines for this hydrogen model , matched the observed spectral lines , it even went as far as to explain why these spectral lines occur . However , another success of the Bohr model was caused by a slight miscalculation . The predicted spectral line included an unknown line that had not be previously observed , this error in calculation caused scientists to look for a new element that this line could be . After vigorous observations an element was found whose spectral line was just a fraction away from that predicted by the Bohr hydrogen model . This element was Deuterium and this was seen as the greatest success of the Bohr model . Using the spectral lines that would be caused by the Bohr model of other elements , scientists were able to predict the existence of numerous other elements , many of which were later found . Further success came from Bohr 's idea that only two electrons can be on the ground energy level with eight needed on higher energy ones . This meant that if there were not eight electrons in the outer orbit , then the element would be unstable . The element would want to become stable and it could do this by gaining , losing or sharing its electrons on the outer energy level so that the number of electrons comes to eight . This process explained the chemical interaction that occurs when two elements meet , with the stable elements being non reactive and unstable elements being more reactive , depending on how many electrons it either needs to gain or lose . This can be seen in water -LRB- H2O -RRB- , which is the chemical interaction of one oxygen molecule and two hydrogen . The oxygen atom has only six electrons in its outer shell and therefore wants to gain two , whereas each hydrogen has one free space . Therefore instead of stealing an electron they simple join together and share the electrons in the outer shells . This idea , predicted which elements would be the most reactive , such as potassium , because these atoms only need to lose or gain one electrons to become stable , and therefore react much easier . Despite all these successes of the Bohr model they were far outweighed by its flaws . Although some of the extra spectral line that were predicted by the Bohr model were later observed , such as Deuterium , many did n't , and there were far too many predicted spectral that had not been accounted for . These flaws were probably due to there being no theory behind the energy levels of each element , with Bohr using quantum numbers that simply matched observations . This meant that Bohr 's idea of energy being absorbed and emitted in the form of a proton , was only accurate for hydrogen atom , which has only one proton and electron with no neutrons , and did n't work for any more complicated elements . This meant that many changes were needed to the Bohr-Rutherford model ; one such change was made by Somerfield , who spent years manipulating the Bohr model in order for it to match the observed calculations . One such scientist was the German physicist Erwin Schrödinger ; he believed that the problem with Bohr 's model might be in confining the electrons to specific orbits . However , as the electrons now behave as waves , scientists could not calculate exactly where they lye and therefore this is only an estimate . To solve this problem instead of having the well defined orbits of Bohr 's model Schrödinger used probability clouds -LRB- orbitals -RRB- , and he could predict where on the orbital the electron will be . Some areas of the cloud had a high probability of having an electron and others a low probability . Schrödinger 's model was very useful as its electron clouds allowed it be able to solve problems that Bohr 's model was not able to do , this allowed scientists to get a better understanding of how electrons behave in chemical reactions . Bohr 's model of the atom became useful despite its flaws , because it 's classical structure is very easy to visualise at a basic level , with the electrons on set energy levels and energy either being absorbed or emitted in the form of a photon . It also introduced the idea of quantum mechanics into the model structure of an atom , which allowed future scientists to improve Bohr 's ideas . Bohr 's model , which structures the atom in a classical way with electrons orbiting the nucleus but behaving using quantum mechanics , with the electrons on set energy levels has become extremely successful partly because of and partly despite its obvious flaws . The amino acids form crystals , whilst the bulkier amino-acids give the spider silk an amorphous structure . It is a combination of the strong crystalline segments and elastic amorphous structure , which provide spider silk with it 's properties . The silk is produced in glands on the spiders abdomen , there are up to seven glands to produce silk for different purposes , however no spider possesses all seven of these glands . Once the silk has been produced it is spun using pairs of spinners on the back of the spider 's legs , a spider could have anything up to five pairs of spinners .
