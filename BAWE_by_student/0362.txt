The pneumatic cylinder converts the potential energy stored in the compressed air into linear displacement . They are typically cylinders hermetically sealed which house a piston that divides the interior into two sections . By applying compressed air into one or both sections the piston moves . You can see this happen as the cylinders rod extends out or contracts into the cylinder . Cylinders are typically round and their internal radius can be deciphered from their part number . Single Acting cylinder : Single acting cylinders have one input air inlet , producing power stroke in one direction only . The pressurized air forces the piston rod out and when the air supply is removed , the air inside the cylinder is allowed to escape . Hence , the piston rod moves back by the force of the spring . By restricting the exhaust , the speed of the return of the piston rod can be controlled . Single acting cylinders are used for compressing , marking , ejecting , etc. . Figure 1 shows the main parts of the single acting cylinder . The ISO symbol for single acting cylinder is . Double acting cylinder : Double acting cylinders have two input air inlet , producing extending and retarding power strokes . The piston rod extends when pressured air is supplied from one inlet and air from the other inlet is allowed to exhaust freely . When air is supplied to second inlet , the first is allowed to exhaust freely , resulting the retarding of the piston rod . This type of cylinder gives greater power on the return stroke than the single acting cylinder , and also ensures that the piston is effectively locked in position at both ends of its stroke . The thrust available during the retardation is reduced because of the less effective piston area . Figure 2 shows the working of double acting cylinder .
