This article attempts to analyze the relevance of concept of ` Developmental States ' and ` Competition States ' in the East Asian States . It firstly defines the meanings of these two concepts . Then it investigates the role of state in East Asia State in the early stage of development and shows that they are the ` Developmental States ' . And it notes that ` Developmental State ' are being transformed in the globalizing world by investigating the international and domestic effect . It therefore concludes that the transformation from the developmental state to competition state is the best description and the change is diverse in different countries due to their different economic structures . In addition , it gives some limitation of the competition state concept . Between 1960s and 1980s , East Asia was the most fast-growing region in the worlds . To explain the growth in this region , the concept of developmental states which emphasizes on the importance of role of states is popularly used . In 1993 , World Bank used this explanation to appreciate East Asian Miracle . But after the Asian Crisis , these states faced the economic recession . In 1997 , the World Bank changed to criticize the outdated state intervention of East Asia . In fact , the international organization changed their attitude , because the state is being changed by globalization . There are hot debates in contemporary IPE concerning the role of states in the globalizing world , and the states in East Asia are the popular case study . In this debate , there are two schools of thought : Hyperglobalizers and the Skeptics . The former -LRB- such as Strange -RRB- finds that the growths of global production and markets cause the retreat of state . And the latter school -LRB- such as Cameron , Garret , Swank , Rodrik & Weiss -RRB- argues that the state increases its intervention -LRB- such as social insurance -RRB- to offset the increase of the risk in the globalizing world . But the former and latter overemphasizes the international force and domestic force respectively . The former ignores the historical background of state including the state-society relations ; while the latter neglects the nature of structure and the contribution of the globalization . Therefore , to study the effect of globalization , we should deploy both International Political Economy -LRB- IPE -RRB- and Comparative Political Economy -LRB- CPE -RRB- into analysis . This paper uses the transformationalist school of thought -LRB- concept of competition state invented by Cerny -RRB- to find the way of state transformation and prevent the prejudice on either decrease or increase of state intervention . But before describing the transformation , the paper defines the concept of developmental state and competition state . Then it shows the way and reason of the transformation of role of state by using IPE and CPE framework and applies into three countries -LRB- Singapore , Korea and Japan -RRB- . Finally , it concludes that the competition state is the best concept to describe current situation in East Asia but it still has some limitations . Apart from United States and Europe , the East Asian States show the successful story of the economic development in the world in the post-war period . Between 1965 and 90 , the average growth of GNP per Capita in these states was about 5 percent which was higher than that amount in the OECD economies -LRB- about 2.5 % -RRB- and the Latin American countries -LRB- about 2 % -RRB- . So the explanation of successful stories in the East Asian States is worth studying . Chalmers Johnson firstly invokes the concept of developmental state to show the importance of Japanese state in the extraordinary post-war economic development . Then , many comparative political economists -LRB- such as Weiss , Haggards , Wade and Evans -RRB- expand the concept of the role of state into other East Asian States including South Korea , Taiwan , Singapore , Hong Kong , Thailand , Malaysia , Indonesia and China . Evans points that the states in this region are ` developmental ' comparing with the ` predatory ' Latin America and Africa . That means the leaders of these states have the commitment to develop their economy after the devastation of World War II . In order to achieve the development purpose , the states promoted the export-led industrialization -LRB- growth -RRB- strategy . They employed a variety of intervention - ` selective protection , subsidies , state-owned enterprises - to achieve their investment , export , and industrial objectives ' . The state , therefore , had the close relationship with the business group to achieve the economic growth . This business and state relationship may cause some state intervention problem such as rent-seeking activity and corruption . But many East Asia study scholars are confident about the state intervention and think that the state intervention problem can be overcome by the state 's capacity . They generate the ideas of 'em bedded autonomy ' -LRB- Evan -RRB- , ` governing the market ' -LRB- Wade -RRB- , and ` governed interdependence ' -LRB- Weiss -RRB- to argue that the state policy is not affected by the societal groups . Therefore , the concept of Developmental State is mainly about the importance of the state capacity in the economic development . When the growth of East Asian States showed the model of ` developmental state ' , another concept called ` Competition State ' was emerging in the global level . This concept is to explain the decrease of state intervention in the global level . In fact , starting from 1980s , Anglo-Saxon states -LRB- such as United States , United Kingdom -RRB- led both of developed and developing world to abandon the idea of embedded liberalism and Keynesian Economic policy which requires the state intervention and to embrace the neo-liberalism which emphasizes on the small government . They carried out several transformations such as trade liberalization , financial deregulation and privatization . To explain this phenomenon , the concept of competition state is used . Many ideas of ` Competition State ' are based on the works of Cerny . He points that the competition state is formed to embrace the transnational dimension , because the intense competition in the global economy driven by flexibility and open Anglo-Saxon Market -LRB- or economic globalization -RRB- has already happened . And this is a new concept of national interest and a way of maintaining national competitiveness in trend of globalization . Every state in the world is undergoing the process of political structuration or globalization . . Under this political structuration , ` the shaping of the playing field of politics itself is increasingly not within insulated units ... . rather it derives from a complex congeries of multilevel games played on multilayered institutional playing fields . '' So the state intervention is undermined , and the state policies have three kinds of change : ' -LRB- 1 -RRB- a shift from macro-economic to micro-economic interventionism ; -LRB- 2 -RRB- a shift from intervention based on comparative advantage to competitive advantage ; -LRB- 3 -RRB- a shift from welfare state to competition state . ' But he thinks that there is ` dialect of divergence and convergence at work , rather than single road to competitiveness ' . The political globalization is the sign of convergence , but it is also a path-dependent process . Different countries decrease the state intervention in different ways and speeds . And the process is moved to multiple equilibrium points . In addition , the concept has some limitations in explaining the extent of competition state . The most competition state , United State , still has and expands some intervention . However Cerny thinks that the government intervention of the competition states is ad hoc . The commodification or marketization -LRB- privatization -RRB- of the state activities and structure rather than decommodification -LRB- provision of welfare -RRB- is a main trend of state 's policy . But , generally , the concept of competition state can explain that the states started to give the authority to the other sector -LRB- such as privatization and deregulation -RRB- in 1980s to seek or fight for the competitiveness . In addition , the concept of Developmental State and Competition States are mutually exclusive . The former emphasizes the importance of the state intervention in economic development . While the latter claims that the less state intervention can make the national economy more competitive . These two opposite ideas should not exist in the same period of time . In fact the Developmental State can be shown in the early development of East Asia . But the concept of developmental state is to explain the success of economic development in East Asia . When the states faced the economic recession caused by globalization , the developmental state concept is outdated . Indeed , the states are transformed into other form . The following sections show the reason and the way of transformation of developmental state in East Asia and conclude that the competition state is formed in the current situation . In the early stage of development , most of the East Asian States ' leaders had the commitment to develop their economies . One of the best examples is founding father of modern Singapore , Lee Kuan Yew . In the 1960s , he committed to develop his state 's economy by imposing the strict law to preventing the corruption . Although the strict law prevents the business affecting the state policy , the Singapore actively worked with them , especially foreign investor . They provided the superb infrastructure such as telecommunications and gave the subsidies such as tax holidays to Multi-national enterprise in order to attract the foreign capital . Therefore , Singaporean government can show the embedded autonomy of the Development State . Other East Asian States also had this concept . Japanese State -LRB- including the Liberal Democratic Party and Bureaucratic -RRB- closely collaborated with the large industrial conglomerates -LRB- Keiretsu -RRB- to drive the economic growth , this relationship is known as ` Iron triangle ' . Because of the legacy of Japanese Colonial development , the Korean States and Taiwanese States also had the close relationship with the large business group -LRB- Chabeol -RRB- and the small and medium size company respectively . Korea provided the state-directed credit to increase private investment and create big industrial conglomerates ; while Taiwan gave the tax credit to private sector . Furthermore , Malaysia , Indonesia and China also follow the Japanese Model because of the economic success of that country . But we should not ignore that some of the countries such as Thailand and Hong Kong are the exceptional case . These two states did not have close relationship with business group . Because the former has the low social cohesive culture and the latter 's colonial government has ` positive non-intervention ' ideology . But their economies are still successful , especially for Hong Kong . Therefore , the concept of developmental state is not applicable to all of the East Asian States . So the problem of the concept of developmental states is its failure in explaining the diversity of state intervention in the East Asia . In fact , this concept is described as institutional fetishism which overemphasizes the domestic state capacity but ignores the other factor in the developmental policy . Jayasuriya points that policy capacity recognizes that ` the state is not an ` entity ' , but a complex and constituted set of relationships between frameworks of political authority and the international political economy , domestic social forces , and the broader ideational notions of authority or stateness . '' Therefore , Thailand and Hong Kong 's exceptional example can be explained by its ignorance of other factor such as international factor . So far , the developmental state concept can explain most of the East Asia States in its early development -LRB- between 1950s and 1980s -RRB- . But it ignores the international dimension . It has the problem of CPE concept which overemphasizes the domestic politics . Starting from 1980s , the international factor is becoming more important . The political globalization has transformed the developmental state into competition states . Competition state concept describes the international effect and domestic effect in state which is more applicable now . So the next section describes the transformation from developmental state to new competition state empirically . East Asia is also a part of world , the concept of competition state is supposed to be applicable in the East Asia . When Cerny mentioned the concept of competition state in case of East Asia in 1990 , he found a certain amount of liberalization and the revaluation of currency in this region , but he did n't think that the international force or globalization can affect the East Asian States to carry out some reducing size project such as privatization and deregulation at this time . Therefore he concluded that competition state is not applicable in this region and the Japanese model -LRB- developmental state -RRB- is still alive and well . In fact , the developmental state started to transform to competition state from 1980s . In 1980s , Singaporean government did n't think that the heavy state intervention can make them competitive . The only way is to reduce the state intervention by carrying out privatization , liberalization and deregulation in 1986 . His mistake may be caused by the unobvious transformation at that time . But after Asian Crisis , several states -LRB- such as Korea , Malysia -RRB- found the problem of excessive intervention and carried out several kinds of restructuring -LRB- such as deregulation -RRB- which made a significant transformation . Many scholars -LRB- such as Hall , Jayasuriya , Pang , and Shaw -RRB- claim the end of state-led development after these events . So we should start to investigate the decrease of state intervention from 1980s and focus the transformation in three countries : Singapore , Korea and Japan . Before 1980s , the Singapore government used the Keynesian economic -LRB- or fiscal -RRB- policy and macro-economic intervention to maintain the economic stability . They interfered in the wage levels of private sector . In order to deal with the inflation caused by oil crisis in 1979 and promote the labor into higher skill level , the Singapore National Wage Council doubled the Singapore 's real wages between 1978 and 1980 . But the large-scale increase of wage eroded the Singapore 's international competitiveness . As the wages increased , the cost of Singaporean goods became less attractive than the other competitive East Asian States ' goods especially for the Chinese goods . Therefore , Singapore faced the problem of recession in the first half of 1980s . Apart from the high wages caused by over-regulation , other heavy state interventions such as high company tax rate , rising statutory charges , and a high Central Provident Fund contribution rate were also the reason of the recession . The state responded to the economic downturn by recognizing the problem of excessive intervention . They think that the reducing of state intervention can maintain the competitiveness . Singapore 's Ministry of Trade and Industry provided three main recommendations : -LRB- 1 -RRB- minimizing regulations to prevent abuse and over-regulation ; -LRB- 2 -RRB- divesting the government from companies that could prosper on their own ; and -LRB- 3 -RRB- relying more on private sector initiatives and enterprises to generate growth . So a number of the measures was introduced by the Singaporean government in 1986 , such as tax concession ; tax incentives and grants for research and development ; a reduction of the Central Provident Fund Contribution rate ; freezing overall wages ; minimizing trade restrictions ; introducing incentives to encourage the fund management industry ; and education improvement . But the two most important measures are : the adoption of the flexible wage system and privatization . In 1987 , the Wage Reform Committee introduced a flexible wage system that emphasized using productivity incentive rather than strict wage control . The privatization was initiated in 1985 . Singaporean government relaxed the role of statutory boards , public corporations and government companies in economic development . In fact , in the early economic development , Singapore did not have enough capital to develop and the local business group was not competitive enough . The state used some intervention , for example , subsidies to attract the foreign capital and establishment of the state-own enterprise to help the development . The intervention was in market-friendly way . But as the economy grew in 1980s , the foreign capital was well-developed as well . The state intervention became less useful and not friendly to market . Therefore , in order to keep the friendly image to the foreign enterprise , the government closed down the State-Owned Enterprise -LRB- SOE -RRB- . As a result , the contribution of SOE to GDP was reduced to less than 10 % in 1995 . . The reduction of state intervention increased the productivity and decreased the production costs for the Singapore 's economy . Between 1974 and 1986 , Singapore had a budget deficit every year . But , since 1986 , its budget surplus has been produced . After 1988 , the economy was recovered and aimed to compete with Hong Kong as a financial centre . But the decrease of state intervention does not stop . For example , after economic recession in 1998 , the Singaporean government found that their wage system is flexible in allowing companies to link performance to rewards , but it is not flexible enough to enable companies to make quick adjustments to their wage costs in response to a sudden and severe business downturn . So the state gave more flexible to private sector to adjust the wage . But the state still controlled 70 % of the wages . Therefore we can see that the Singapore government has the strong reason in decreasing its intervention to maintain its competitiveness in global economy . The concept of national interest in Singapore has been expanding to embrace the private sector i.e. transnational dimension , because the multinational enterprise is relatively strong -LRB- comparing with domestic sector -RRB- in Singapore 's economic structure . The Korea 's transformation is mainly caused by the Asian Financial Crisis . Pirie thinks that the crisis stemmed from the changing in the global production structure , the irrationality of Korean State and rise of global financial market . For the production structure , he finds that the transportation cost decreases globally and Research and Developmental -LRB- R&D -RRB- cost increases in Korea because of the economic globalization . But the Korean state still irrationally invested in the high cost of R&D . Apart from it , the state also allowed that major conglomerates -LRB- chaebol -RRB- which had already relied heavily on debt finance to grow their liability . At the same time , as the capital market liberalization , the financial market is vulnerable to be attacked by foreign capital . The poor performing business is easily attacked by the speculator . Similar with the Singapore 's economic recession , the crisis is the opportunity for Korea to change . After the crisis , the International Organization -LRB- IMF -RRB- and the Korea leader started to think that Korea has to eliminate the close relationship between state and business . Former Managing Director of the IMF , Michel Camdessus criticized the Korea government which did not invite market friendly policies and interfered in markets . ' . Kim Dae-jung who just elected as Korea president after the crisis committed to change the business-government relations . There are two main transformations in state intervention in Korea -- Central Bank Independence and the creation of independence financial regulator On 31 December 1997 , under the influence of IMF and the reformer of Korean State , the new Bank of Korea -LRB- BOK -RRB- Act was passed . This act enhances the authority and autonomy of national monetary authorities -LRB- central bank -RRB- . It is a significant shift in macroeconomic policy-making responsibility away from politicians to autonomous technocrats . Furthermore , the Governor of the BOK who is a civil servant replaced the Minister of Finance and Economy as head of the MPC after this act . The effectiveness of central bank reform should be coupled with a comprehensive a corporate governance system . The FSC is famous to impose budget constraints on the two -LRB- Daewoo and Hyundai -RRB- of the five major chaebols because of their refusal of corporate restructuring -LRB- such as the merger and acquisition -RRB- . East Asian States and Latin American States both had the state intervention in the market . But the difference was the state behavior or attitude in the economic development . East Asian states had the commitment to adopt shared growth policy during the developing process . Share growth policy is to equally distribute the fruit of growth to the public . The East Asian States have been successful in achieving shared growth . All of the Asian States increased in education expenditure in order to bring equal chance for the general public . The last successful ingredient of the East Asia state is its flexible policy . The inconsistent of state intervention explained by the neo classical and statist view was happened in one country , because both of them only examine the intervention in the certain historical period . On the other hand , the unsuccessful Latin American states can be explained by its rigidity in changing the import-substitution strategy . Therefore , the theories of developmental states are the kind and quality of states intervention . The core characteristics of East Asian states are their embedded autonomy , and their shared growth , market enhancing or market-friendly and flexible states policy .
