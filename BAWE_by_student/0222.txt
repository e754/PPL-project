The opportunity to sell to global markets rather than a single national market increases the potential profitability of business activity . Today , every organisation wants to have presence in the foreign markets , either to expand its operation and make use of economies of scale production or to protect the home market from domestic and foreign competition . There are different ways in which a company can do international business such as exports , licensing , joint ventures , mergers , FDI , etc. . Many nations now consider international trade essential for economic growth and development . As a result , most of the countries have lowered most of their trade barriers to have an open economy for trade . Export can be considered as a first step to enter any foreign market . Currency risk is the risk that the Group suffers financial loss as a result of changes in the value of an asset or liability or in the value of future cash flows due to movements in foreign currency exchange rates . -LSB- 3 -RSB- Centrica group has operational exposure in US and Canadian dollars and euros through its subsidiaries in US , Canada and Europe . The Group 's policy on Net Investment hedges and operating Cash flow hedges are explained as under : The Group 's policy is to use balance sheet hedging instruments for maintaining the sterling value of its foreign currency investment . Canadian dollar and euro balance sheet translation exposure is hedged by maintaining and approximating the foreign currency financial liabilities to the net asset value of the Canadian and Euro based operations . This is done by holding either foreign currency debt or entering into foreign currency derivatives or a mixture of both . Thus , changes in the sterling value of borrowings or derivatives are used to offset changes in the sterling value of the investments . Similarly , US dollar balance sheet translation exposure is hedged by borrowing on a short-term basis through a US commercial paper programme . -LSB- 3 -RSB- The Group 's policy is to use forward foreign exchange contracts to hedge the risk of future foreign currency cash flows variation with changes in foreign exchange rates by fixing the sterling value of a specified foreign currency amount on a specified basis .
